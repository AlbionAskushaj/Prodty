# Loot pools

Design draft for the wizard's gear. All numbers are placeholders to tune once the app is playable.

## Ground rules

- Nothing is bought. Gear drops from finished tasks or is earned through feats.
- Embers are the crafting currency: reroll a trait, upgrade a piece, level the wizard.
- No timers that punish. Nothing expires, resets or is lost by being away.

## Slots

| Slot | Kind | What rolls |
|---|---|---|
| Staff | Weapon | Stats and two traits |
| Flame | Weapon | Stats and two traits |
| Lantern | Armour | Stats |
| Coat | Armour | Stats (also sets gloves and boots) |
| Scarf | Armour | Stats |
| Keys | Charm | Stats |

## Stats

| Stat | Effect |
|---|---|
| Spark | Embers earned per task |
| Fortune | Drop chance and drop tier |
| Grit | How fast bounties grow |

Each piece rolls a total split at random across the three stats. A lopsided roll (all Spark, say) is as valid as an even one.

| Tier | Stat total per piece | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 12 to 18 | Any task |
| Rare | 20 to 28 | Any task |
| Epic | 30 to 38 | Any task, and the Hoard |
| Legendary | 40 to 50 | Only the source named on its set |
| Mythic | 60, fixed | Only the feat ladder of its set |

## How drops work

| Wizard's appraisal of the task | Chance of a parcel |
|---|---|
| Trivial (cross off one list item) | 2% |
| Small (under 10 minutes) | 10% |
| Medium | 25% |
| Large (an hour or more) | 50% |
| Bounty | 100% |

- The wax seal colour shows the tier before the parcel is opened.
- Duplicates are expected. The same design with a better roll is the chase. Unwanted pieces are dismantled for embers.
- Drops lean slightly toward slots you are missing in a set you have started.
- **The Hoard** fills with everything you do and holds a guaranteed Epic or better when full. It never resets.

## Tree affinities

Trees are custom, so loot is tied to an affinity, not a tree name. The wizard suggests one when a tree is planted and it can be changed.

| Affinity | Covers | Example tree | Legendary set |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study | Courses, learning, exams | School | Archivist |
| Discovery | Research, experiments, open questions | Research | Stargazer |
| Making | Side projects, code, building | Side project | Tinker |
| Hearth | Home, errands, life admin | Personal | Hearthkeeper |
| Body | Health, movement, sleep | Health | Trailwalker |
| Kin | People, replies, calls, favours | Friends and family | Courier |

Six more sets come from how you work, whatever the tree:

| Source | Legendary set |
|---|---|
| Clearing bounties | The Watcher |
| Finishing a whole branch | The Harvestman |
| Chains (tasks finished within an hour of each other) | Stormglass |
| Long tasks (appraised at an hour or more) | Deepwater |
| Tasks finished after dark | Night Moth |
| Letting go, and coming back after time away | The Wisp |

## Sets, bonuses and Mythic

- **Legendary pieces** drop with random stats. Staffs and flames roll one trait from the general pool and one signature trait of their set.
- **Full set (all six pieces, Legendary or Mythic):** the set bonus switches on and the wizard transforms.
- **Mythic pieces** come from the set's feat ladder: six rungs, one piece per rung, in the order Keys, Scarf, Coat, Staff, Flame, Lantern. They have fixed top stats and you choose their traits yourself.
- **The Mythic lantern** is the last rung and carries a capstone trait found nowhere else.
- **Full Mythic set:** the transformation gains its ascended details and the set bonus doubles.

Feat ladders:

| Set type | Ladder (six rungs) |
|---|---|
| Affinity sets | Embers earned in trees of that affinity: 500, 1,500, 3,500, 7,000, 12,000, 20,000 |
| The Watcher | Bounties cleared: 5, 15, 30, 50, 75, 100 |
| The Harvestman | Branches finished: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21 |
| Stormglass | Full chains reached: 10, 30, 60, 100, 150, 200 |
| Deepwater | Long tasks finished: 5, 15, 30, 50, 75, 100 |
| Night Moth | Tasks finished after dark: 25, 75, 150, 250, 400, 600 |
| The Wisp | Times you told the wizard what really happened (changed, done differently, dropped): 10, 30, 60, 100, 150, 200 |

## General trait pool

Rolled on any staff or flame.

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Early Flame | +25% embers before noon |
| Bounty Hunter | Bounties pay +30% |
| Kindling | The first task after opening the app pays double |
| Clean Sweep | Finishing every leaf on a branch drops a bonus parcel |
| Lucky Find | +5 Fortune on trivial and small tasks |
| Deep Pockets | Dismantled gear returns +50% embers |
| Slow Burn | Tasks older than a week pay +20% |
| Fresh Start | The first task in a newly planted tree drops a parcel |
| Odd Jobs | Tasks that fit no tree pay +25% |
| Keen Eye | Parcels have a small chance to upgrade one tier when opened |

## The twelve sets

### 1. Archivist (Study)

A librarian who has read everything and remembers where it is.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Reading Lamp | Green glass banker's shade on a brass arm |
| Flame | Study Light | Steady green-gold glow that never flickers |
| Coat | Card-Catalogue Coat | Tweed with elbow patches; the pockets are tiny labelled drawers |
| Scarf | Ribbon Bookmark | A long red silk bookmark ribbon |
| Staff | The Long Quill | An oversized fountain pen with a split nib |
| Keys | Reading Room Keys | Brass keys on a stamped library tag |

- **Signature traits:** *Underline* (staff): the first Study task you finish each day drops one tier higher. *Slow Reader* (flame): tasks appraised over 30 minutes pay +20%.
- **Set bonus, Marginalia:** every fifth Study task drops a guaranteed parcel.
- **Transformation:** the dialogue box becomes a ruled index card, he adds dry footnotes, and his text blips are typewriter clacks.
- **Ascended details:** loose pages drift around whichever tree you are viewing.
- **Capstone, Perfect Recall:** leaves in Study trees never fade.

### 2. Stargazer (Discovery)

An astronomer who treats every open question as a new star.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Armillary Cage | Nested brass rings that turn slowly |
| Flame | Pole Star | A small, hard, white star |
| Coat | Night-Lined Coat | Plain outside, a constellation map as lining |
| Scarf | Comet Tail | Pale and long, trailing off into flecks |
| Staff | Spyglass Staff | A collapsing brass telescope |
| Keys | Sextant Keys | Keys hung from a small sextant |

- **Signature traits:** *First Light* (staff): the first finished task on a brand-new branch pays +30 embers. *Parallax* (flame): open-ended tasks get +25% Fortune on their drop.
- **Set bonus, Orbit:** finishing tasks in three different trees in one day drops a parcel.
- **Transformation:** thin gold constellations appear over the forest, one star per finished task, and he speaks with calm wonder.
- **Ascended details:** the constellations slowly join into a picture unique to your history.
- **Capstone, Alignment:** Orbit parcels are always Legendary or better.

### 3. Tinker (Making)

A workshop engineer who would rather ship than polish.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Welder's Mask | Riveted iron with a slit visor |
| Flame | Pilot Light | A small blue-white jet |
| Coat | Shop Apron Coat | Scuffed leather with tool loops |
| Scarf | Oil Rag | A stained red rag, knotted |
| Staff | The Big Wrench | A wrench as tall as he is |
| Keys | Hex Keys | A ring of Allen keys |

- **Signature traits:** *Ship It* (staff): finishing the last leaf on a branch guarantees a parcel. *Iterate* (flame): rerolling a trait costs half the embers.
- **Set bonus, Spare Parts:** dismantling gear returns double embers.
- **Transformation:** small gears turn in the corners of the dialogue box, sparks fly when a leaf blooms, and he speaks in clipped reports ("Shipped.").
- **Ascended details:** a tiny clockwork helper follows him and carries the parcels.
- **Capstone, Prototype:** one free full reroll of a piece, recharging weekly.

### 4. Hearthkeeper (Hearth)

The one who keeps the house warm and the small things done.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Copper Kettle | A dented kettle with the flame showing through the spout |
| Flame | Hearth Fire | Warm orange with drifting sparks |
| Coat | Patchwork Housecoat | Quilted squares, no two alike |
| Scarf | Hand-Knit Scarf | Far too long and slightly uneven |
| Staff | The Good Broom | A hearth broom with a fire-poker tip |
| Keys | House Keys | Worn keys on a wooden fob |

- **Signature traits:** *Tidy As You Go* (staff): trivial and small tasks each add to a shared parcel that opens every ten. *Kettle's On* (flame): the first task after opening the app pays double.
- **Set bonus, Warm Hearth:** the Hoard fills 25% faster.
- **Transformation:** steam puffs from the kettle when he is pleased, it whistles on a drop, and his speech turns gentle.
- **Ascended details:** a thin line of chimney smoke rises somewhere in the forest.
- **Capstone, Open House:** a full Hoard always holds at least one Legendary.

### 5. Trailwalker (Body)

A hiker who is always halfway to somewhere.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Trail Lantern | A storm-proof lamp with a wire guard |
| Flame | Steady Beam | White-gold, pointing forward like a torch beam |
| Coat | Waxed Trail Jacket | A rope coil at the hip and sewn-on patches |
| Scarf | Sun-Faded Bandana | Knotted at the collar |
| Staff | Summit Pole | A walking pole with an ice-axe head |
| Keys | Compass and Whistle | Hung where the keys would be |

- **Signature traits:** *Second Wind* (staff): after a Body task, your next task in any tree within two hours pays +50%. *Fresh Air* (flame): a Body task doubles the drop chance of the next task.
- **Set bonus, Base Camp:** Body trees never go quiet.
- **Transformation:** he cannot stand still, pacing and stretching, and his speech is brisk.
- **Ascended details:** a footpath wears into the forest between the trees you visit most.
- **Capstone, Tailwind:** Second Wind lasts for the rest of the day.

### 6. Courier (Kin)

A postmaster who believes no message should go unanswered.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Pillar-Box Lantern | A red post box; the flame peeks through the letter slot |
| Flame | Sealing-Wax Flame | Rose-red, dripping wax |
| Coat | Postmaster's Greatcoat | Brass buttons and a letter satchel |
| Scarf | Airmail Ribbon | Striped along the edge |
| Staff | Post Horn | A staff topped with a brass horn |
| Keys | Postbox Keys | Small keys with a stamp for a tag |

- **Signature traits:** *Return Post* (staff): every third Kin task drops a parcel of Rare or better. *Kind Word* (flame): tasks that involve another person pay +20%.
- **Set bonus, Special Delivery:** Kin parcels have a chance to hold two items.
- **Transformation:** the dialogue box becomes a letter with today's postmark, and he signs off his messages.
- **Ascended details:** a folded-paper bird circles him and carries the parcels in.
- **Capstone, Chain Letter:** whenever a Kin parcel drops, the next task in any tree drops one too.

### 7. The Watcher (bounties)

What he becomes when you keep facing the things you dodged.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Crown of Spikes | A tall black iron cage with a jagged crown |
| Flame | The Unsleeping Eye | A slit-pupil eye of red and orange fire |
| Coat | Warden's Black Coat | High jagged collar, pointed shoulder plates, torn hem |
| Scarf | Red Tatters | Blood-red and shredded |
| Staff | The Iron Claw | Twisted black iron ending in a claw |
| Keys | Gaoler's Keys | Heavy and dark |

- **Signature traits:** *Dread Tithe* (staff): bounties grow twice as fast. *Stare Down* (flame): doing a bounty straight after "What now?" offers it pays +40%.
- **Set bonus, Dominion:** clearing a bounty doubles the chance its parcel is Legendary.
- **Transformation:** he stands taller, his blips drop to a low rumble, and his wording turns ominous ("It is done. Another falls.").
- **Ascended details:** forest shadows lean toward him, and the eye follows your finger around the screen.
- **Capstone, All-Seeing:** you can see the tier and slot of a bounty's parcel before you clear it.

### 8. The Harvestman (finished branches)

A scarecrow who only cares about what gets brought in.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Turnip Lantern | A carved grin, the only set that gives him a face |
| Flame | Candle Grin | Amber glow behind the carving |
| Coat | Straw-Stuffed Coat | Patched, with straw at the cuffs |
| Scarf | Burlap Wrap | Rough sacking tied with twine |
| Staff | The Gleaning Scythe | A long-handled scythe |
| Keys | Seed Pouch | A drawstring pouch and a small sickle charm |

- **Signature traits:** *Reaping* (staff): finishing a branch drops two parcels instead of one. *Last Sheaf* (flame): the final leaf on any branch pays triple.
- **Set bonus, Fallow:** a finished branch leaves a seed, and a branch planted from it pays +10% on every leaf.
- **Transformation:** the carved face changes expression with his mood, and he speaks in a slow drawl.
- **Ascended details:** crows perch on his shoulders and fallen leaves swirl at his feet.
- **Capstone, Bumper Crop:** branch parcels roll twice and keep the better result.

### 9. Stormglass (chains)

A lighthouse keeper who works best when the weather is up.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Hurricane Lamp | A ship's lamp with a wire cage |
| Flame | Bottled Lightning | A white bolt trapped in the glass |
| Coat | Oilskin Coat | A long yellow-grey sou'wester |
| Scarf | Gale Scarf | Always blowing sideways |
| Staff | Lightning Rod | Topped with a weathervane |
| Keys | Ship's Bell | A small brass bell |

- **Signature traits:** *Chain Lightning* (staff): each task finished within an hour of the last adds +10%, stacking five times. *Static* (flame): at a full chain, drop chance doubles.
- **Set bonus, Squall:** reaching a full chain drops a parcel.
- **Transformation:** his speech is fast and clipped, with thunder under the blips.
- **Ascended details:** the weather follows your pace, with ink rain sweeping in during a chain and clearing when it ends.
- **Capstone, Eye of the Storm:** a chain holds for three hours instead of one.

### 10. Deepwater (long tasks)

A diver who goes down for a long time and comes back with something.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Diving Helmet | Brass with a round porthole |
| Flame | Angler's Lure | A teal light on a stalk outside the helmet |
| Coat | Canvas Dive Suit | Heavy canvas with lead weights |
| Scarf | Kelp Wrap | A length of dark kelp |
| Staff | The Sounding Line | A harpoon with a coiled rope and lead weight |
| Keys | Anchor Keys | Barnacled, on a small anchor |

- **Signature traits:** *Pressure* (staff): long tasks drop one tier higher. *Held Breath* (flame): a long task finished in one sitting pays +30%.
- **Set bonus, Fathoms:** long tasks always drop a parcel.
- **Transformation:** bubbles rise from the helmet, and his text wavers and arrives with slow pauses.
- **Ascended details:** the forest light turns blue-green and fish silhouettes drift between the trees.
- **Capstone, Sunken Treasure:** the longer the task, the more stat points its drop rolls, with no cap.

### 11. Night Moth (after dark)

The version of him that only really wakes up at night.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Paper Moon | A round folded-paper lantern |
| Flame | Crescent Light | A pale silver crescent |
| Coat | Moth-Wing Cloak | Grey-violet with eye-spot patterns |
| Scarf | Silver Gauze | Thin and nearly see-through |
| Staff | Reed and Moon | A reed with a small paper moon hanging from it |
| Keys | Crescent Keys | Silver, curved like the moon |

- **Signature traits:** *Night Shift* (staff): tasks finished after dark pay +25%. *Drawn to Light* (flame): the first task after dark drops a guaranteed parcel.
- **Set bonus, Quiet Hours:** after dark, Common drops are upgraded to Rare.
- **Transformation:** he whispers, with smaller and softer text and blips, and the forest is always shown at night.
- **Ascended details:** moths gather round his lantern and scatter whenever a leaf blooms.
- **Capstone, Midnight Oil:** after dark, every drop rolls one tier higher.

### 12. The Wisp (letting go and coming back)

What is left when he stops carrying things that no longer matter.

| Slot | Piece | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern | Broken Cage | Bent bars, door hanging open, nothing inside |
| Flame | Free Flame | A green flame that floats above the cage and wanders |
| Coat | Unfinished Coat | Drawn in outline only, never inked in |
| Scarf | Mist Ribbon | Fades to nothing at the ends |
| Staff | Bare Branch | A branch with one leaf that never falls |
| Keys | Skeleton Keys | Keys with no teeth |

- **Source detail:** a parcel when you let go of a task you have held for two weeks or more, and a welcome-back parcel after a week or more away.
- **Signature traits:** *Unburdened* (staff): letting a task go refunds half its bounty in embers. *Welcome Back* (flame): your first task after a week or more away pays triple and drops a parcel.
- **Set bonus, Clean Slate:** when you come back after time away, every quiet tree wakes at once.
- **Transformation:** he floats with his feet off the ground, speaks most quietly of all, and never mentions the past.
- **Ascended details:** leaves you let go drift over to him and he pockets them.
- **Capstone, Nothing Lost:** a task you let go leaves a seed, and planting it later brings the task back with its old bounty.

## Open questions

- Ladder numbers and drop chances are guesses and need tuning against real use.
- Night Moth rewards working late. Decide whether "after dark" should follow the clock or a time you set.
- The Wisp rewards letting go. The two-week rule is there to stop tasks being added and dropped for loot.
