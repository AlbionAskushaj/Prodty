# The Dark

Design draft for the optional game beyond the forest. Numbers are placeholders to tune in play.

## Purpose and limits

- A place to use the gear earned from real tasks, with real skill and build choices.
- Optional. Nothing in the forest depends on it.
- Hard cap of five minutes per run.
- Real life stays the only source of power. The Dark gives style, tuning and story.

## Getting runs

- Every finished task gives one flask of oil. Nothing else does.
- Flask size follows the task's effort: about 90 seconds for a tiny task, up to the full five minutes with a keeper for a large task or a bounty.
- At most three flasks can be banked.
- Small tasks earn at most three flasks a day (they still pay embers).
- No timers refill oil, and there are no daily quests in the Dark.
- After a run the wizard returns to the forest. There is no "play again" button.

## Core play

One thumb, one virtual stick.

- **Push the stick far:** he walks, and the lantern casts its wide pattern. You see what is near you but the light barely hurts.
- **Pull the stick in:** he plants his feet and the light focuses in the direction you aim. It destroys shades but everything outside it goes dark.
- **Tap:** the staff's cast, on a cooldown.

## Oil

Oil is the timer and the health bar.

- It drains steadily.
- A hit from a shade burns some.
- Focused light burns it faster.
- Kills return a little.
- Flasks found in the Dark can top it up, never past five minutes in total.
- Running out ends the run. He walks home with everything he found.

## Skill

- **Flare:** every shade shows a tell before it lunges (its eyes widen). Putting focused light on it during the tell kills it instantly and refunds oil.
- **Aiming:** focused light goes where the stick points.
- **Positioning:** a tree or wall at your back stops anything approaching from behind.
- **Personal bests:** each zone has depths of rising difficulty, and the best run per zone is recorded. No leaderboards.

## Feel of a kill

- The shade cracks, then tears into ink flecks.
- A paper-rip sound and a tap of vibration.
- A split-second freeze on each kill.
- An ink stain stays on the ground for the rest of the run.
- Oil flies back to the lantern.

## Stats in the Dark

| Stat | Effect |
|---|---|
| Spark | Brightness: damage of focused light and size of the wide pattern |
| Grit | Oil lost per hit |
| Fortune | Hidden finds the light reveals |

Deeper zones and depths expect a higher gear total.

## Shades

All nine are in the first version.

| Shade | Behaviour | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Blot | Slow and basic | Any focused light |
| Skitter | Fast swarms that circle behind | Wide patterns |
| Lurker | Frozen while lit, moves only in the dark | Keep it inside the wide pattern |
| Snuffer | Puts the light out for three seconds if it reaches you | Kill it at range first |
| Glint | Its mirror bounces focused light away | Flank it, or bank the light into other shades |
| Flutter | Drawn to brightness, drinks oil | Dim builds |
| Sipper | Latches on and siphons oil | Walk to shake it off |
| Husk | Armoured shell | A staff cast cracks it, then focused light |
| Inkwell | Spawns Blots until lit for two seconds | Find it and hold light on it |

## Builds

A build is three choices plus armour stats: the lantern sets the shape of the light, the flame its effect, the staff the cast.

| Lantern | Set | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Lantern | Starter | Circle when walking, cone when standing |
| Reading Lamp | Archivist | A steady pool in front with no warm-up; shows each shade's weak point |
| Armillary Cage | Stargazer | Three thin long rays that pierce through everything |
| Welder's Mask | Tinker | A very short, very bright arc; highest damage, stuns |
| Copper Kettle | Hearthkeeper | No beam; a ring that grows the longer you stand |
| Trail Lantern | Trailwalker | Strikes while walking, but weaker |
| Pillar-Box Lantern | Courier | A wide flat bar that sweeps a whole line |
| Crown of Spikes | The Watcher | A narrow gaze; anything looked at freezes, then dies |
| Turnip Lantern | The Harvestman | Light through a carved face; shades caught in the grin return oil |
| Hurricane Lamp | Stormglass | Charge while standing, release a bolt that chains between shades |
| Diving Helmet | Deepwater | A spotlight steered at a distance from the body |
| Paper Moon | Night Moth | So dim shades ignore you; one large silent flash on a long charge |
| Broken Cage | The Wisp | You steer the flame itself while the body waits in the dark |

## Zones

| Zone | Twist | Builds that shine | Keeper (draft) |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Thicket | None; the starting zone | Anything | The Stag: charges in straight lines; its weak point shows only in wide light |
| Fogmarsh | Fog halves the wide pattern | Deepwater, Stargazer | The Ferryman: hides among false lights |
| Gale Ridge | Wind shrinks open flames while you walk | Stormglass, Trailwalker | The Kite: circles overhead and dives, gusts push you |

| The Stacks | A buried library; open flame burns the loot | Archivist, Night Moth | The Censor: covers parts of the screen in black bars |

## Rewards

- Ink dyes to recolour gear.
- Reroll material for tuning a piece.
- Pieces of the wizard's past, found deeper in.
- Beacons that stay lit and mark how far you have pushed.

## Tie to deadlines

When a big deadline is missed, his flame gutters and the Dark is closed until you tell him what happened and settle it: done late, a new date, or let go.

## Build order

The Dark is roughly as much work as the rest of the app. It comes after the core loop works. All nine shades and all thirteen lanterns are in scope for its first version; zones can arrive one at a time, starting with the Thicket.
