# Lore

The story of Prodty. Told in fragments over months of play, never as a block of text.

## The world

Prodty was lit by the Great Flames, living fires as old as mountains. The Lantern Kingdoms caged them to power their cities and went to war over who owned them.

## The general

The wizard was the greatest general of that war, undefeated, with a name whole armies chanted. He pushed the front further than anyone ever had.

## Wick

- A village lamplighter's son, brilliant and kind, the best student the general ever took. "Wick" was the general's pet name for him.
- While he was at the front, his home city's flame was pulled away to feed the war. The city went dark with his parents and his younger sibling in it. It was nobody's cruelty. It was the war they were both fighting.
- He decided it happened because he was not strong enough. He stopped sleeping and drove his soldiers the same way. He studied forbidden lamp-craft, drawing light out of living prisoners. He began to say a Great Flame could reverse death and that anyone unwilling to try was a coward.
- The general saw all of it and looked away, because an obsessed apprentice wins battles. He praised the drive and ignored the grief. This is his real guilt.

## The last siege

Wick reached into the dying last Great Flame to take it for himself. The general pulled the ember out to save it, and that is what killed Wick. Both accounts are true: "I saved the last flame and lost my boy" and "my master took the light and let me burn."

The general put the ember in his own lantern and vanished. The histories say he killed the Flame. The Flames went out, and the Dark came.

## The Dark

- The Dark is where the people of Prodty wake when their light goes out. They wake as shades: drifting, hollow, without memory.
- Wick woke with his mind whole, because a sliver of the Great Flame was still in him.
- He found his family there, as shades who do not know him.
- He learned to give shades their minds back, and raised the dead of the old company as keepers.
- He began the Bridge, a crossing from the Dark into the living world, so that no one would ever lose anyone again. He needs the wizard's ember to finish it.
- His lantern is the inverse of his master's: it casts a beam of shadow that erases light.

### Why it cannot work

Only the dead with something left undone can be woken. His soldiers had unfinished business. His family died whole and at peace, so nothing he does will reach them. The wizard knows this, and Wick cannot hear it.

## Him

As ruler of the Dark he has no name. The wizard only ever calls him "him". The name Wick is learned from letters found in the Dark, and the wizard does not say it aloud until the end.

## The wizard now

A wanderer who brews tea at the edge of the world and keeps one ember alive. He learned that a Great Flame never fed on tribute or fear. It feeds on things honestly finished.

## You

The first student he has taken since Wick. That is why he never pushes or guilts you: he has seen what pushing costs. Every task you finish feeds the ember, and one day it becomes a Great Flame again.

## Two philosophies

Wick stands for getting things done by force, fear and obsession. The wizard stands for finishing things honestly and gently. Wick speaks to you directly now and then and tries to recruit you.

## The company

The twelve sets were the general's company.

- Six survived as hermits and send gear your way: the Archivist, Stargazer, Tinker, Hearthkeeper, Trailwalker and Courier.
- Five are lost in the Dark as keepers: the Harvestman, Stormglass, Deepwater, Night Moth and the Wisp.
- The Watcher set is the general's own war regalia. Wearing all of it brings his old voice back. Wick models himself on it: he became the man his master used to be.

## How it shows up in play

- **Shades** are the lost dead. Destroying one releases it.
- **Keepers** are not killed. They are relit and relieved of duty, and their gear comes home.
- **Big deadline bosses** are crossings: things he sends over the Bridge where dread has thinned the border.
- **A missed big deadline** means a crossing got through, and the wizard holds the border until you settle it.
- The Dark never advances because you were away.

## The ending

You beat Wick and release him, and he finally finds peace. The app does not end there.

- The ember becomes a Great Flame, which is the Lighthouse rank.
- Wick's Lantern becomes the final Mythic: the only lantern that casts a beam of shadow.
- The Dark stays open with harder depths, as a place to explore and no longer a threat.

## The wizard's name

His true name is **Taper**: the thin candle used to light other candles, which passes its flame on and gets shorter doing it. He named Wick as the one he meant to pass his flame to.

- He has two names. The world knows his war title (draft: "the General of the Thousand Lanterns"), and Wick sneers it at him. Taper is the humble name only Wick ever used.
- You name him in the first five minutes and he takes the name warmly.
- He hints it is not his: a half-second pause before he answers to it, a scratched-out inscription on old gear, a keeper who starts to address him and is cut off.
- The reveal comes after Wick is defeated. As he is released, Wick's last word is "Taper", said with love for the first time since the siege. The wizard answers "Wick", a name he has never said aloud until then.
- Afterward he offers you the name, and admits he prefers the one you gave him.

## How it is told

Letters between master and apprentice found in the Dark, a keeper's last words, and a rare line from the wizard as he ranks up. He tells you the kind version first. The truth comes later.
